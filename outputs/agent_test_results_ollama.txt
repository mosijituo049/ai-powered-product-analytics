TEST CASE 4: Definition question
======================================================================

Question:
What does checkout abandonment mean in e-commerce?

Final response:
Checkout abandonment refers to the percentage of customers who start the checkout process but do not complete their purchase. In other words, it is the number of cart abandonments divided by the total number of sessions where a customer has added items to their cart.

Based on the data provided by Google Analytics 4 (GA4), the checkout abandonment rate for your e-commerce application is 56.33%. This means that out of all the sessions where a customer has started the checkout process, 56.33% did not complete the purchase.

The total number of abandoned sessions is 6246, and the average session duration is approximately 2990 seconds (or about 50 minutes). The abandonment rate can be used to identify areas in the checkout process that may be causing customers to abandon their purchases, such as difficulty with payment processing or long shipping times.
