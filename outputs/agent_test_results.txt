TEST CASE 4: Definition question
======================================================================

Question:
What does checkout abandonment mean in e-commerce?

Final response:
Checkout abandonment refers to the percentage of customers who start the checkout process but do not complete a purchase on an e-commerce website. In other words, it's the number of cart contents left behind by users without making a payment or completing their purchase.

In this case, our data shows that:

* There were 11,088 checkout sessions.
* Only 4842 of those sessions resulted in a purchase (i.e., the customer completed the checkout process).
* Meanwhile, 6246 customers abandoned their shopping carts without making a payment (leaving 5,542 cart contents behind).

The abandonment rate is approximately 56.33%, indicating that nearly six out of ten customers who began the checkout process did not ultimately complete a purchase.

This data can be used to identify areas for improvement in the e-commerce website's checkout process, such as reducing friction or increasing trust with customers.
